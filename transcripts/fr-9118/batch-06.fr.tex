% Nothing on these three views is text. They are the end of the binding, and
% no line of them is transcribed. What each one is, read from the mirror:
%
%   view 101 — an opening at the very end of the book. On the left, the verso
%     of the last leaf of the volume, blank: what shows on it is offset from
%     its own recto (view 100), the mirrored ghost of a few lines and of the
%     round BnF stamp, and not writing on this side. On the right, the rear
%     free endpaper, blank, with the turn-in of the board along the fore-edge.
%     A pencil paraph or figure sits on the fold near the gutter, one stroke,
%     unreadable at full resolution and not a foliation; the faint marks at
%     the bottom-left of the frame are on the backing sheet under the leaf,
%     not on the paper. No pencilled folio number is legible on either side.
%   view 102 — the rear pastedown and the inner face of the lower board:
%     marbled paper edge to edge, no writing, no bookplate, no shelfmark.
%   view 103 — the outside of the lower board: marbled paper boards with a
%     leather spine and corner. The spine's gilt lettering is caught only in
%     fragments at the edge of the frame and is the binder's, not the
%     manuscript's; a scalloped corner label carries the figure 9.
%
% So: no \page, no \folio, no \ill, no formula. The volume's text ends at
